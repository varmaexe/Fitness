## Form Notes

**Machine Tricep Extension (Preacher) — "Let me know if I should continue"**
Yes, keep it. The preacher arm pad locks your upper arm in place and eliminates shoulder involvement — that's a genuinely different stimulus from the cable overhead extension (long head in stretched position) and the pushdown (peak contraction). Three exercises covering three distinct positions on the strength curve is solid tricep programming. Just make sure you're not accumulating so much tricep fatigue that pushdown performance tanks. Current placement in the session order is fine.

**Seated Incline Curl — "Only 5 reps on right. Is my right arm weaker or is form wrong? Idk" + "Shoulder elevating in last reps"**
Both. It's a real strength asymmetry *and* a form breakdown — they're feeding each other. The whole point of the incline position is to place the shoulder behind your torso and maximally stretch the bicep long head. The moment the shoulder rises off the pad, that stretch is gone and you've converted it into a half-rep standard curl, recruiting anterior delt to compensate. You're not getting the stimulus you came for.

Fix: **drop the right arm to 8kg next session**. Left stays at 10kg. Before each rep, consciously pin the back of your shoulder into the pad. If you feel the shoulder start to float, that rep is done — rack it. Do not grind through 5 partial reps with bad form. Once you can hit 3×6 with the right arm cleanly at 8kg (no shoulder lift), move back up to 10kg.

**Hammer Curls — Right arm dropping to 12.5kg from set 2**
Same pattern as Nov 11 (shoulder rising on right arm, last 3 reps noted). Two sessions in a row now — this is established, not a one-off. The right brachialis fatigues faster than the left and the right trap/shoulder kicks in to rescue the rep. Do not push through it. Use separate starting weights: 12.5kg right arm, 15kg left arm, from the first set. Once the right arm can complete 3×8 at 12.5kg with clean form and zero shoulder elevation, then match it to the left at 15kg. The asymmetry only closes if you stop letting the compensated reps count.

**Universal rule for right arm:** Always start unilateral exercises with the right arm. Whatever rep count you get with the right arm — that is the rep count for the left arm. Do not let the left side accumulate extra volume. That's what closes gaps.
