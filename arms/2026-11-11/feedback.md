**"Right shoulder is raising up a little while doing right arm. Last 3 reps in all hammer curl sets" — Standing DB Hammer Curls:**
Classic compensation pattern. When the brachialis or bicep fatigues, the upper trap and anterior delt kick in to initiate the curl via shoulder elevation. This is both a form breakdown and a signal that your right arm hits local muscular failure before left does. It will cause rotator cuff irritation over time if uncorrected. Alternatively, control the shoulder with your left hand on your right shoulder as a tactile cue — feel it and stop it.

**"Just tried new workout. Couldn't reach full ROM due to weight" — Hammer Curls Preacher Machine:**
This is the wrong way to introduce an exercise. Full ROM is not optional for hypertrophy — partial ROM means partial stimulus. You should have immediately stripped weight until ROM was achievable. At 20kg you couldn't finish the movement. Next session: drop this exercise entirely (it's redundant), or if you want it in a future session, start at 10-12.5kg and build ROM first.
