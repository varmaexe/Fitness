## 4. Form Notes

**Bulgarian Split Squats — Apr 7 note: "More challenging in lower back than quads"**
This is a hip-hinge diagnosis. When BSS loads the lower back instead of quads, the most common cause is **forward trunk lean** — the torso tips over the front knee. Fix: keep your front shin more vertical (don't let the knee shoot too far forward past the toes, but also keep your chest tall). Your front foot placement matters: if the foot is too close to the rear bench, you're forced into a forward lean. Try moving the front foot 3–5cm further forward and actively squeeze your rear glute throughout the movement. You should feel it burning in your front quad and glute. If you feel lower back dominant, stop the set and reset foot position.

Today's session at 12.5kg had no form notes logged — which either means the cue from April was fixed, or you weren't paying attention to it. Next session: explicitly monitor this cue on every rep.
