## Top 3 Priorities for Next Week

### 1. 🦵 Program and complete a leg session by Nov 14
Squats or leg press, Romanian deadlifts, leg curls — minimum 10–12 working sets. No more skipping legs during a regain phase. Leg training also creates systemic anabolic stimulus that benefits your push/pull work.

### 2. 📋 Log every meal for 7 consecutive days
Doesn't need to be perfect. Even rough tracking reveals whether you're hitting surplus and protein targets. Without this, the lean-bulk phase has no feedback loop. Target: **~79.9 × 16–17 kcal/lb adjusted, ~160 g protein minimum**.

### 3. 🔁 Hit PULL twice before next Sunday (Nov 15 and Nov 18)
PULL is the most underdeveloped movement relative to baseline (32%). Two pull sessions this week — one heavier compound-focused (rows, weighted pull-ups), one higher volume — will accelerate the regain curve and close the gap before the phase switch back to cut.
