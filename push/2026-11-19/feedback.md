## 4. Form Notes

**"Form didn't feel right" — Seated Machine Fly 30kg (dropped to 25kg)**
Right call to drop weight, but the lack of specificity makes it hard to fix. Likely causes: shoulders elevating/shrugging during the eccentric, arms leading instead of chest, elbows too high, or no scapular set before initiating. Fix protocol for next session: sit tall, retract and depress scapulae before unracking, chest up and out, slight elbow bend locked throughout. Start at 25kg, 3×8, slow 3-second eccentric. **"Didn't feel right in seated machine fly" → pivoted to Cable Crossover mid-session**
Understandable impulse, but this is a problem. When you can't feel an isolation exercise, the solution is to lighten the load and fix the mechanics — not to switch exercises while already fatigued. By the time you got to cable crossover, you'd already done incline press, dips, shoulder press, and machine fly. The stabilisers were cooked. That's why the cramp happened.

**"Muscle cramp in shoulder blade/lower trap region" — Cable Crossover, Set 2**
This is the most significant note of the session. A cramp in the lower trap/scapular area during cable crossover almost always comes from one of three things:
1. **Scapular elevation under load** — shoulders creeping up during the movement, overloading the upper trap and stressing adjacent lower trap
2. **No scapular set before initiation** — blades moving freely throughout the arc instead of staying anchored
3. **Accumulated stabiliser fatigue** — after 3 heavy pressing movements, the scapular stabilisers have nothing left for a cable fly

Before every cable crossover rep: "chest up, shoulders down and back" — hold that position for the entire arc, don't let the shoulder blades wing. If this cramp recurs even with correct setup, drop to 10kg and treat it as a technique drill. Do not push through a cramp in this region.

**"Only BW" — Dips, last set (dropped plate for final set)**
Expected. The target next session is 5kg across all 4 sets. If you can't complete 4×8 at +5kg, do 4×6 with the plate rather than 3×8 + 1×BW. Dropping the plate is a step backward.
