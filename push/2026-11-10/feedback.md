## 4. Form Notes

> **Lateral Cable Raises – Set 1:** *"Just spammed reps. Mostly clean reps."*
> **Lateral Cable Raises – Set 2:** *"Spammed reps."*

This is a problem. Lateral raises are one of the most technique-sensitive exercises in a push session — the medial delt is a small muscle and momentum completely bypasses it, transferring load to the traps and upper chest. "Spamming" 18 reps at 5kg with momentum is effectively a cardio set, not a hypertrophy stimulus. 

**Fix this immediately:** 2-second concentric, 3-second eccentric, full stop at the bottom. If you can't do that at 5kg, drop to 4kg. The goal is controlled, painful reps — not a high rep count. 12 controlled reps at 5kg will do more than 18 swings. Cap sets at 15 reps max; if you hit 15 clean, go to 6kg next session.
